# Reading Notes (Sample of 100 Threads)

## Recurring Issue Types
1. **Delivery Delays / Missing Updates**: Customers frequently complain about packages not arriving on the promised date or missing tracking updates.
2. **Missing / Stolen Packages**: Packages marked as delivered but not found by the customer.
3. **Account & Payment Issues**: Questions about Prime membership charges, declined payments, or inability to log in.
4. **Order Cancellations / Refunds**: Customers wanting to cancel orders, complaining about unauthorized cancellations, or asking for refund status.
5. **Product / Service Inquiries**: Questions about Prime Video availability, Kindle products, or specific item stock.

## How the Brand Resolves Issues
- **Information Gathering (Secure Link)**: For almost all specific order inquiries, the brand uses a canned response asking the customer to use a secure link or call/chat directly since they cannot handle account specifics over Twitter.
- **Clarification**: Often asking "What is the expected delivery date?" or "Who is the carrier?"
- **De-escalation / Apology**: Responses generally start with "I'm sorry for the trouble" or similar empathy before requesting details.
- **Privacy Warning**: If a customer posts an order number or email publicly, the brand immediately tells them not to share personal info publicly.

## Surprising / Notable Observations
- **Multilingual Support**: The dataset contains a significant amount of non-English text (Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese) handled fluently by the brand's regional support accounts.
- **Privacy enforcement**: The brand actively polices the customer's security, admonishing them for posting order numbers or emails in public tweets.
- **Sarcasm/Frustration**: High frustration levels are common (e.g., "Wth is the point of paying for amazon prime...", "This is unacceptable. Are you in mood of cheating?").
- **Positive Engagements**: There are some purely positive interactions where customers share photos of pets with Amazon boxes, or books they bought, and the brand replies conversationally.
