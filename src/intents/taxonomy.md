# Intent Taxonomy

## Scope
This taxonomy defines the intents used to classify customer support inquiries directed at AmazonHelp. 

## Intents

### 1. DELIVERY_DELAY
**Definition:** The customer is complaining or inquiring about a package that has not arrived by the promised date or is missing tracking updates.
**Examples:**
- "Where is my package? It was supposed to be here yesterday."
- "My tracking hasn't updated in 3 days."

### 2. MISSING_PACKAGE
**Definition:** The package is marked as delivered by the carrier, but the customer cannot find it or claims it was stolen.
**Examples:**
- "App says delivered but it's not on my porch."
- "Someone stole my package from the lobby."

### 3. ACCOUNT_PAYMENT_ISSUE
**Definition:** The customer is experiencing issues with their account login, Prime membership, or payment methods.
**Examples:**
- "Why was I charged $139 for Prime?"
- "My credit card keeps getting declined."

### 4. CANCELLATION_REFUND
**Definition:** The customer wants to cancel an order, is asking about the status of a refund, or is complaining about an unauthorized cancellation.
**Examples:**
- "Cancel order #123-456789."
- "I returned my item last week, where is my refund?"

### 5. PRODUCT_INQUIRY
**Definition:** The customer has a question about a specific product, stock availability, or a digital service like Prime Video or Kindle.
**Examples:**
- "When will the PS5 be back in stock?"
- "My Kindle won't turn on."

### 6. POSITIVE_FEEDBACK
**Definition:** The customer is sharing a positive experience, thanking support, or posting pictures of their items/pets with Amazon boxes.
**Examples:**
- "Thanks for the quick help!"
- "My cat loves the new Amazon box."

### 7. OUT_OF_SCOPE
**Definition:** The inquiry does not fit any of the above categories, is too vague to classify, or is not an actionable support request.
**Examples:**
- "Wow."
- "I like turtles."
